Sources and assumptions

This is a separate experiment. Your Combined game is unchanged. The starting plots represent unfinished restoration, not a copy of your saved board.

Point clouds come from measured scans. Crop placement, growth, income and fire are simulated. Managed shade and weeding reduce risk here; agroforestry is not fireproof and is not counted as restored native forest.

The association has secure access and has agreed to the work. Income pays local care first. No carbon-credit payments or continuing grants are assumed. Real results depend on markets, labour, land rights and weather.
