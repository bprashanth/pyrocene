About this experiment

Aim to close three new canopies in twelve years. Strategies are advice, not different rules. Weather stays the same when you replay this landscape.

Measured forest scans provide the points. Plant placement, growth, budgets and fire are simulated. Six-month turns compress restoration for play; they are not predictions. Credits are game money, not carbon credits.

Forest neighbours help recovery through shelter and seed supply. However, young planting can still burn. This is a simplified introduction to assisted natural regeneration.
