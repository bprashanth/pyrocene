Sources and assumptions

This is a separate experiment. Main-game rooms and saves are unchanged.

The scan is measured. Species placement, livelihoods, costs and fuel conditions are a fictional scenario. No historical burn scar is reconstructed.

Pink shows lower vegetation, not proof of an invasive species. Inspect plants and ground conditions.

The shared pot starts with 4 credits: 2 for restoration and 2 from the community. The nursery investment is additional; payment for its plants is already within the restoration cost. Feed savings and future fruit sales are not cash for today's restoration.

Nursery plants are raised off-map for A. Cupuaçu uses B. Grazing uses C. Neither pasture nor a fruit plot can also be native restoration on the same land.

Grazing feed savings are fixed in this short scenario, assuming suitable regrowth and cattle access; they are not a real-world guarantee. Cupuaçu income is a qualitative scenario tied to nearby shelter, not a yield forecast.

Surface fire starts from a single point. Fuel, moisture and wind shape its spread. Recovery assumes continued care. Damp forest can still burn in worse drought.
