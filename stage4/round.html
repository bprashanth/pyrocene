About this trial

Measured forest points. Invented patches, budgets and projected recovery. Money is in game credits, not carbon credits.

The same spark and wind drive both fire runs. This is a teaching model, not a forecast. Projected growth reuses measured canopy points. It is not a prediction for these species.

Each team inspects a patch and submits one proposal. The room reveals both and commits once. Team links stay valid until the server restarts.
